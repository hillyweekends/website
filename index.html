Why it feels so hard right now

The hidden cost of modern work

As humans, our brains simply were not designed to handle the massive number of decisions and tasks we face every day.

When we plan our day, we usually pick tasks based on how much time or physical effort they take. What we completely ignore is the heavy mental processing required to actually do them.

When you constantly ignore that mental drain, you end up exhausted, stuck, and overthinking.

Why you can't just "fix it" yourself

People often try to coach themselves out of a rut, but it rarely works. Why? Because you cannot see your own thinking from the outside. When you are inside your own head, you are in the thick of it. You can't see where your thoughts are going or where they are getting trapped.

Coaching is like renting somebody else's brain for a little while.

It offers you a dedicated time and space to focus on exactly one thing. By stepping away from the noise and talking with a coach, you can think much deeper than you ever could on your own.

About Me

I bring 24 years in the corporate world to every conversation. That means I have sat where you sit. I have felt the pressure, navigated the politics, and wrestled with the same questions that keep you up at night.

My coaching is grounded in the ICF methodology, and I am actively working toward full certification. The framework is rigorous, but the real value is simpler: I know how to ask the question that unlocks the answer you already have.

Beyond the office, I have spent 15 years running nonprofits, both internationally and here in Canada. That taught me how to lead without authority, build trust across cultures, and hold space for people under real stress.

I have also led groups deep into the wild as a hike leader. There is something about guiding people through uncertain terrain that translates directly to coaching. You learn to read the room, spot the moment before someone quits, and help them find their next step.

When I am not coaching, you will find me on a dance floor learning Bachata and Salsa, out on the water on a stand-up paddleboard, or disappearing into the trails with a backpack. These are not hobbies. They are how I stay grounded, curious, and connected to the same energy I hope you will rediscover in our sessions.
